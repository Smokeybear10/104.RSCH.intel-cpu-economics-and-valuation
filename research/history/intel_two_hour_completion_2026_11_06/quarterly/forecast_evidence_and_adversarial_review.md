# Quarterly forecast: evidence and remaining limits

The selected forecast is complete arithmetic, with sixteen quarterly CPU and client revenue observations, explicit unit and ASP indices, and annual reconciliation. It is not a measurement of Intel's physical capacity. The old CPU target remains a credible favorable execution case.

## What changed and why

CPU 2027 starts at the existing $6.526 billion Q4 estimate. Sequential unit assumptions are 0%, 5%, 10%, 10%, and ASP assumptions are 0%, 2%, 3%, 3%. This puts most growth after the first half. It produces $6.526/$6.989/$7.919/$8.972 billion quarterly sales.

The timing has evidence; the exact rates are analyst judgment. The [Q3 prepared remarks](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_47986fe477a6ac84fe34efbcbf38dd13/intel/db/887/9273/prepared_remarks/Q3%2726_Prepared_Remarks.pdf) describe improving Intel 3 output, supply shortages extending into 2028, and a Q4 guide already reduced for Fab 28. The [Q3 call, 32:22–33:01 and 42:07](https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2026-10-29-intel-co-stock/) places much of the substrate expansion and Oregon Intel 3 production in the second half of 2027. At 39:00 management expects similar Fab 28 revenue effects in Q1; at 55:02 it declines to provide a quarterly DCAI guide. Flat Q1 is therefore an owned cautious allowance, not management guidance.

The [July Ireland announcement](https://newsroom.intel.com/intel-foundry/intel-invests-5-billion-euro-to-expand-manufacturing-in-europe) identifies a €5 billion program using existing facilities, equipment installation and campus infrastructure. It supports a real manufacturing expansion, but does not disclose finished-package quantities or quarterly acceptance. The page redirected during direct access; the indexed Intel release and [Intel's Ireland overview](https://www.intel.ie/content/www/ie/en/company-overview/intel-in-ireland.html) were available. No wafers-per-month estimate from discussion forums is used.

After 2027, selected CPU unit growth slows to 3% per quarter in 2028, 2.5% in 2029 and 2% in 2030. ASP rises 0.75%, 0.5%, 0.5% per quarter respectively. These rules were selected before the agent saw revised equity values. They yield annual unit growth of 22.33%, 11.17%, 9.03%. The earlier annual 20%/15%/10% rule is retained as a control: distributing it smoothly within years required sequential growth to accelerate from 2.22% in 2028 to 4.33% in 2029. That reacceleration had no specific delivery evidence. The selected path avoids hiding it.

## The old forecast is possible

Holding selected H1 sales and the ASP path, $31.475 billion in 2027 CPU sales needs quarterly unit growth of 0%, 5%, 16.97%, 10%. H2 units would be just 6.34% above the selected case. That is a favorable ramp, not an impossible one.

Under the chosen historical calibration, exact annual 20% unit and 20% ASP growth requires H2 units 25.78% above H1 and weighted ASP 5.65% higher. The result is close to the favorable schedule, but the calibration is not identified by the filings. Ireland expansion, substrate additions, product launches and constrained demand are reasons this case deserves weight. No public customer schedule or capacity conversion proves it.

[Oracle's October 27 roadmap](https://www.oracle.com/news/announcement/blog/ai-world-building-the-next-decade-of-oracle-cloud-infrastructure-2026-10-27/) confirms planned Diamond Rapids X14 offerings alongside NVIDIA and AMD alternatives. The [joint release](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/newsroom/news/corporate/intel-oracle-collaborate-on-enterprise-and-agentic-ai.html) describes intentions and coordinated capacity planning; it publishes no accepted-unit, price or shipment timetable. Customer validation supports the demand story without identifying our revenue.

## Client recovery has a separate mechanism

Selected 2027 client units change -5%, +10%, +15%, +5% sequentially, with ASP 0%, +1%, +1%, +1%. This assumes inventory correction wanes and regular seasonality returns. The Q3 call at 48:20 distinguishes OEM inventory normalization from recovery in the PC market; memory conditions remain difficult. The selected Q4 client sales of $6.339 billion remain below Q3 2026's rounded $6.9 billion.

The old CCPG target requires H2 client sales 42.64% above selected H1. At the selected ASP path, one compatible schedule has Q3 units +30.23% and Q4 +5%. A strong inventory rebound and Nova Lake uptake could do that; it is not independently confirmed.

[TrendForce, October 8](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20261008-13264.html), projects a low-single-digit 2027 notebook decline, with high-single-digit downside if costs pass through. This is less severe than Intel's later roughly 10% whole-PC outlook, with different scope and timing. [TrendForce, September 4](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260904-13217.html), describes purchases pulled into H1 2026 and a 53:47 H1:H2 notebook shipment split. [IDC, June 2](https://www.idc.com/resource-center/blog/pc-market-enters-volatile-territory-as-memory-shortage-persists-through-2027/), also identifies pull-forward and persistent memory pressure; its system ASP estimate is not an Intel chip ASP estimate. [AMD's Q3 filing](https://ir.amd.com/financial-information/sec-filings/content/0000002488-26-000191/amd-20260926.htm) reports client units up 6% versus Intel's reported decline, a reason not to assume Intel automatically matches market recovery.

For 2028–30, annual client unit growth of 5%, 5%, 3% uses quarterly unit weights of 23%, 24%, 26%, 27%. In 2028 this requires a visible 14.67% Q1 decline from the strong Q4 2027 exit. It is an owned seasonal reset, not an unexplained annual haircut. There is no automatic restoration of missed 2027 sales.

## Historical precision is limited

The [Q1](https://www.intc.com/filings-reports/all-sec-filings/content/0000050863-26-000079/intc-20260328.htm), [Q2](https://www.intc.com/filings-reports/all-sec-filings/content/0000050863-26-000157/intc-20260627.htm) and [Q3](https://www.intc.com/filings-reports/all-sec-filings/content/0000050863-26-000191/intc-20260926.htm) reports disclose quarterly and cumulative unit/ASP changes, not shipment levels. Conditional common-scope rounding constraints do not identify one history. Treating rounded YTD Other DCAI $3.3 billion as exact makes the original $15.332 billion CPU allocation incompatible with the Q3 unit/ASP bands. A feasible rounded history changes the annual revenue allocation by about $39.8 million, immaterial to the pitch but material to claims of precision.

The selected quarterly dollar forecast does not depend on these historical unit weights. Its derived 2027 annual split does: chosen CPU +16.03% units/+19.89% ASP and client -16.95% units/+1.63% ASP are calibrations, not disclosed observations. The model preserves the old annual dollar anchor for comparison and labels the discrepancy.

## What remains unproved

- Finished packages Intel can deliver after wafer yield, packaging, substrates, test and customer acceptance, by quarter and product family.
- Net realized ASP after mix, discounts and input costs. Larger core counts are already inside package ASP and must not be multiplied again.
- The duration of industry CPU-unit growth after 2027. [TrendForce's October CPU forecast](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20261022-13279.html) is near-term; its [November server report](https://www.trendforce.com/research/download/RP261102UN) describes server boxes. [IDC's 2030 outlook](https://www.idc.com/promo/servers/) is primarily dollars, and the [DOE report](https://www.energy.gov/documents/united-states-data-center-energy-usage-report-2025-update) covers US server equipment and changing socket configurations. None identifies Intel's 2028–30 package path.
- The [November 5 TrendForce public abstract](https://www.trendforce.com/research/download/RP261105JR3) supports continued CPU supply constraints and competition. Its paid report was not purchased or accessed.
- [TrendForce's power analysis](https://insights.trendforce.com/p/us-data-center-power-gap) distinguishes shipment-implied demand from grid-deliverable capacity and notes that on-site generation can reduce the difference. It is a reason to stress later deployment timing, not a direct CPU sales haircut.

The operating forecast can now be tested against quarterly results. More arithmetic does not turn the remaining delivery and retention judgments into proof, and the research does not establish a probability of winning M37.

## Important anchor sensitivity

The exact Q4 2026 anchor is itself a forecast. With all subsequent recovery assumptions unchanged, a CCPG Q4 decline of 21%, 25% or 30% produces 2027 CCPG revenue of $28.390, $26.922 or $25.087 billion. The old 2027 client target can be reached with a 20.553% Q4 CCPG decline, consistent with the qualitative greater-than-20% guide. Thus the apparent need for a very strong 2027 rebound depends materially on how deeply Q4 resets.

Illustrative DCAI Q4 growth of 13–17% and Other DCAI growth of 25–45% produce 2027 CPU sales of $29.071–31.739 billion at the selected sequential path. The old $31.475 billion target lies within this scenario grid. These are analyst scenarios, not statistical bounds or precise management guidance. A valuation stress must also update Q4 profit, cash and 2027 opening liquidity; merely changing future sales would be incomplete.

This is a reason to preserve the old forecast as a favorable case and to identify the next reported quarter as a real catalyst for resolving the forecast. It is not a reason to choose whichever anchor gives the desired stock recommendation.
